    How it works

    1. In TikTok, open a video or photo post, tap Share, then Copy link.
    2. Paste the link above and press Get.
    3. Choose MP4, Photos or MP3, select Standard or HD, then download.

    FAQ

    Is Ax Download free?

    Yes. There is no account, no limit on file size and no payment.

    What is the difference between Standard and HD?

    For videos, HD is the higher resolution and bitrate file when TikTok provides one. For photos, HD is the original image and Standard is a lighter 720 px JPG.

    Why does my link fail?

    The post may be private, removed or region-locked. Copy the link again from the TikTok app and retry.

    Can I reuse downloaded content?

    Downloads are meant for personal use. Respect the creator's rights and do not re-upload their work without permission.
